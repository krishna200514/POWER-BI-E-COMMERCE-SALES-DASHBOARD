# POWER BI E-COMMERCE SALES DASHBOARD
## An interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze ecommerce sales performance, customer behavior, profit trends, and payment preferences.
#### The dashboard provides business insights through KPI cards, category-wise sales analysis, state-wise revenue comparison, monthly profit trends, top customer analysis, and profitable product identification.

## Dataset used
- <a href=https://github.com/krishna200514/POWER-BI-E-COMMERCE-SALES-DASHBOARD/blob/main/Details.csv>Details Dataset</a>
- <a href=https://github.com/krishna200514/POWER-BI-E-COMMERCE-SALES-DASHBOARD/blob/main/Orders.csv>Orders Dataset</a>

## Business Questions Solved

* Which product category generated the highest sales?
* Which states contributed the most revenue?
* What is the overall profit generated by the business?
* Which months were most profitable or loss-making?
* Which payment method is most preferred by customers?
* Who are the top customers based on sales amount?
* Which product sub-categories generated the highest profit?
* What is the average amount spent by customers per order?
* How do sales and profits vary across different regions?
* How can business performance be monitored using KPIs and visual analytics?

#### Business Insights

* Clothing was the highest-performing product category in terms of sales contribution.
* Maharashtra generated the highest revenue among all states.
* Cash on Delivery (COD) was the most preferred payment method among customers.
* Printers and Bookcases were the most profitable product sub-categories.
* Monthly profit analysis helped identify profitable and loss-making periods.
* The Average Order Value (AOV) was approximately ₹876 per order.
* The dashboard helps analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and business profitability.
